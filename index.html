About the event

Earth's mightiest coders needed

ASSEMBLE is a 24-hour hackathon organised by the GeeksforGeeks Student Chapter at Bennett University. Just like the Avengers, you'll team up with other heroes to solve real problems and build something amazing.

Pick a track (we call them Infinity Stones), form your squad and start building. We'll have mentors, food, games and a lot of fun throughout the night.

No capes required. Just code!

FAQ

Got questions?

If your question is not here, contact us on Instagram.

Who can participate?

Any student of Bennett University from any year or branch can participate.

What is the team size?

You can participate alone or in a team of up to 4 members.

Is there any registration fee?

No, registration is completely free. Food and swag are also free.

I am a beginner, can I still join?

Yes! Mentors will be there to help you. It's a great place to learn.

What do I need to bring?

Your laptop, charger, college ID card and lots of energy.
